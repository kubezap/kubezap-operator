# KubeZap Brand Guide

## Core identity

**Name:** KubeZap

**Primary tagline:** Kubernetes-native workflow automation.

**Concept:** An isometric cube representing Kubernetes/containerized infrastructure, cut through by a lightning bolt representing triggers, automation, speed, and action.

## Color palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Slate | Deep slate | #243447 |
| Secondary Blue | Muted blue | #4F718A |
| Accent Gold | Muted amber/gold | #C89B3C |
| Light Background | Off-white | #F5F6F4 |

Use the gold primarily as an accent. The logo should remain recognizable when the accent is removed.

## Typography

Recommended web/document fonts:
- Inter
- Montserrat
- Poppins

Use a bold weight for **KubeZap** and regular/medium weight for supporting text.

## Logo variants

- `kubezap-horizontal.svg` — primary general-purpose logo
- `kubezap-horizontal-tagline.svg` — website/marketing contexts
- `kubezap-vertical.svg` — presentations, README hero areas, square-ish placements
- `kubezap-vertical-tagline.svg` — presentation/marketing contexts
- `kubezap-icon.svg` — favicon, app icon, GitHub avatar
- `kubezap-monochrome.svg` — printing and constrained-color contexts
- `kubezap-white.svg` — dark backgrounds

## Usage

- Maintain clear space around the icon equal to at least 1/4 of the icon width.
- Do not stretch or skew the logo.
- Do not add shadows, gradients, bevels, or outlines.
- Do not substitute the gold with a highly saturated yellow.
- Prefer the primary horizontal mark for normal product/docs usage.
- Use the icon alone where the available space is too small for the wordmark.

## Tagline

The recommended tagline is **Kubernetes-native workflow automation.**

The shorter phrase **Turn events into action.** is a good secondary marketing line, but is intentionally not baked into the core logo package.
